About Tsepaya Phone Repair

Tsepaya Phone Repair is a local phone repair business focused on fast, reliable and affordable service.

We also buy old or broken smartphones for cash and sell phones to customers according to their budget, from R400 and up.

🚫 NO ILLEGAL OR STOLEN DEVICES
We do not promote or support any illegal or criminal activity. We only buy devices where the seller can provide solid proof that the device belongs to them.

Proof of Ownership Required

  • A picture of yourself holding the device.
  • Your original name and surname.
  • Your home address.
  • A voice note confirming that the device belongs to you and is not stolen.
  • Any additional ownership information or proof requested during the transaction.

If a device is suspected or confirmed to be stolen, the transaction will be refused and the matter may be reported to SAPS or the relevant authorities.

🧾 Receipt provided: A receipt will be issued as proof of the transaction. For phone sales, the receipt will include the customer's signature, and a copy will be kept by Tsepaya Phone Repair for our records in case a dispute or problem occurs.

📱 Phones for Sale

We sell phones at different prices to suit different budgets, starting from R400 and up. A receipt is provided for every sale.
